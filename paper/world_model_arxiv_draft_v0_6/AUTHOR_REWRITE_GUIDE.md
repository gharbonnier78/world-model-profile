# Author Rewrite Guide — arXiv Draft v0.2

This package is intentionally a **draft to appropriate and rewrite**, not prose to publish verbatim.

## What is frozen

The accepted v0.4 framework spine is frozen. Do not change its scientific meaning casually:

- `L0[rho_pol]`
- `L1_int`
- `L2.G`
- `L2.O`
- `L3`
- acquisition-policy exposure remains an attribution diagnostic inside L0
- finite claims remain only `L0 ⇏ L2` and `L2.G ⇏ L0`

The manuscript may explain, motivate, compare, and add new illustrations around that spine.

## Rewrite method

For each section:

1. Read the cited primary sources.
2. Close this draft.
3. Rewrite the argument from memory in your own technical language.
4. Re-derive every equation you retain.
5. Mark each paragraph privately as one of:
   - imported result,
   - reproduced finite result,
   - framework proposal,
   - interpretation,
   - open question.
6. Re-open the draft only to check missing logic.
7. Send the rewritten section back for mathematical and claim-level review.

## Sections where your own voice matters most

- Introduction: why a binary `has a world model` statement bothered you.
- Eliminativist section: why you might still abandon the term.
- MMALS section: the connection to history compression, regime change, REUSE/ADAPT/FORK.
- Conclusion: what scientific question you actually want to pursue next.

## Do not inherit blindly

The draft is intentionally cautious but still contains choices of emphasis made by the assistant. In particular challenge:

- whether `world-model profile` is the right name;
- whether the historical Craik / Conant-Ashby lineage belongs in the final version;
- whether the L2.O toy is too trivial for the main text;
- how much MMALS should appear in a general arXiv paper;
- whether the AI-assistance disclosure belongs in the paper or only repository metadata.

## Mandatory before submission

- primary-source check of every reference and theorem statement;
- prior-statement search for the mixture-diameter / `delta_inv <= delta_alias` observation;
- independent review of the rewritten manuscript, not only of this generated draft;
- rerun all executable evidence from the final repository;
- ensure no claim silently became stronger during rewriting.
